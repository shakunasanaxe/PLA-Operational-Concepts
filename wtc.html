The concepts at work in the Western Theatre

The Western Theatre Command faces India along the whole of the Line of Actual Control. These examples come from Takshashila Discussion Document 2024-07 on operations and jointness in the theatre, and each is tied back to the entry it illustrates. They rest on PLA media reporting, so they show what the PLA chooses to say about its training, which includes failures as well as successes.
