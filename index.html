How the PLA thinks it will fight

Thirty-eight operational concepts from Chinese military writing, each with a plain definition, the Chinese term with pinyin, the sources it rests on and, for the larger ideas, a diagram drawn for this site. The aim is to make the vocabulary of informatisation, systems confrontation and intelligentisation readable to someone who does not yet read the originals.

About the entries

Reading with care

The definitions paraphrase the six sources listed on the Sources page, and each entry names the sources it draws on. Where a source describes an aspiration or an experiment and not a settled capability, the entry says so.

Chinese terms are given in the standard renderings. Where a concept has no single settled Chinese label in the sources, the entry leaves the Chinese blank and says so, and the English name should then be read as an analyst's label.
